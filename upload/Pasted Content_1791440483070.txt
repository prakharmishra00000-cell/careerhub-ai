You are a senior product architect, UX/UI designer, full-stack engineer, database architect, DevOps engineer, search engineer, and AI product engineer.

Build a complete production-grade web application called:

"CareerHub AI"

Tagline:
"Every Opportunity. One Smart Search."

The platform is a premium job, internship, placement, apprenticeship, fellowship, scholarship, and career-opportunity discovery platform.

The core idea is:

Users should be able to search and discover opportunities from multiple legitimate external sources/platforms in one unified interface.

Potential sources may include:
- LinkedIn
- Internshala

- Naukri

- Startup career pages
- University career portals
- Apprenticeship portals
- Other permitted job/internship feeds and APIs

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT implement unauthorized scraping of websites that prohibit it.
Create a modular source-integration architecture supporting:
- Official APIs
- Licensed feeds
- Partner APIs
- Public feeds
- Permitted crawling where legally/technically allowed
- Manually imported job feeds
- Company-provided jobs

Every opportunity must preserve:
- Original source
